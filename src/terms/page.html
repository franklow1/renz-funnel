What the Style Solve is

Ninety nine pounds buys four things, and nothing else:

  • A forty five minute call, one to one.
  • Your written style document.
  • Your scan, your measurements and your colours.
  • Access to The Fitting Room, for as long as it exists.

It is advice and information. It is not a shopping service, we do not buy anything for you, and no clothing or alteration is included. Anything you choose to buy afterwards is between you and whoever you buy it from.

Who runs the call

Your call is run by Lorenzo or by someone on his team, trained by him to the same method. You are told who you are speaking to when you book.

The call is recorded

We record Style Solve calls so we can write your document properly rather than take notes at you while you talk. You agree to this when you book. If you would rather we did not record, say so at the start of the call and we will not. See our privacy page for how long recordings are kept.

Paying

The ninety nine pounds is paid when you book. Your slot is not held until it is paid.

Moving or missing your call

  • More than 24 hours before: tell us and you can move it, free, as many times as you sensibly need.
  • Less than 24 hours before, or you do not turn up: the ninety nine pounds is not returned. You are welcome to book again at the normal price.

The time is held for you and nobody else can have it, which is why a missed slot is a missed slot. If something genuinely serious happened, write to us and we will use our judgement.

The Straight Back Guarantee

You decide whether it was worth it. At the end of your call, Lorenzo asks you outright. If you say it was not worth it, you get every penny back and you keep your document, your scan and your place in The Fitting Room. You do not give a reason and you do not fill in a form.

Two things to be clear about, because they are the whole shape of it:

  • You are asked on the call, at the end, and that is when you answer. The guarantee is settled there and then.
  • Once the call has ended and you have said it was worth it, that is the decision made.

Money is returned to the card you paid with, normally within two to five business days depending on your bank.

Your legal right to cancel

Because you are buying at a distance, you normally have 14 days to change your mind and get your money back, and nothing on this page takes that away from you.

There is one exception the law allows, and it is worth understanding. If you ask us to run your call inside those 14 days, you are asking us to start early. When you book you confirm you want us to go ahead, and you accept that once the call has been delivered in full, the 14 day right is used up. Until the call happens, you can still cancel for a full refund, whatever the 24 hour rule above says.

If your call is more than 14 days away, none of this applies. The normal right stands.

We will tell you what fits you, which colours are yours and what to reach for. We cannot promise what anybody else thinks of you, what happens in a meeting, or any particular result in your life or your work. Nothing here is a promise of income or opportunity.

Nothing in these terms limits our liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, for fraud, or for anything else the law does not let us limit. Beyond that, our liability to you is limited to the ninety nine pounds you paid.

The Factory Line, specifically

So there is no confusion about what you are buying. The Factory Line is a list of nineteen suppliers you can order single pieces from on DEWU and Taobao, six more that deal only in bulk, and a guide to getting your order to the United Kingdom.

  • It is not a list of the factories that make clothing for named brands, and we do not claim any connection between the two.
  • Nothing in it is made to measure.
  • These are independent suppliers. We do not control what they stock, what they charge, how they size things, or how long they take.

Getting an order to the United Kingdom usually means using a forwarding service. Superbuy is one we have used. We have no arrangement with them and we receive nothing if you use them, their rates change, so compare a few before you choose. Any customs, import or handling charges are yours to pay.

Your photos, and your scan

The intake asks for photos of your face in daylight and of outfits you already own. You tick to consent before you upload anything. You do not have to upload any of it, though we cannot read your colouring without the face photos.

The body scan is carried out by Esenca and is handled under their terms and their privacy policy rather than ours. When you start the scan you are using their service.

We never show your photos to anyone. They are not used in marketing, on social media, on this website or anywhere else, unless you have given us permission in writing for that specific use. Permission for one thing is not permission for everything, and you can withdraw it whenever you like.

Your photos and your intake data are deleted one year after the last time we worked with you. If you want them gone sooner, email and ask and we will delete them and confirm when it is done. All of this is handled under UK GDPR.

Your scan: how long you have, and how many goes

  • You have fourteen days from buying your call to complete your scan, as long as your booking date allows for it.
  • You can attempt the scan as many times as you need. The first two attempts are included.
  • From the third attempt onwards, each one is £10.
  • If a scan fails, your fourteen days starts again from that attempt, so a failed scan never costs you your window.

If the scan is not completed inside that window, the call counts as a missed call and the rule above applies. If your fourteen days has run out and you still want the call, write to us and we will put it back. We are not in the business of keeping money for a call nobody had.
