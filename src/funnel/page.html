    Straight answers

    What men ask me before they book.

    Is this just a capsule closet with a different name?

    No. A capsule closet is someone else’s list of thirty things. It fails for one reason. It was written without you in the room. This starts from your measurements and your colouring and works outwards. Two men who go through this end up with different closets, because they are different men. That is the whole point of it.

    What actually happens in the scan?

    I send you a link. You stand in front of your phone in what you are already wearing, fully dressed. Turn round once. It takes about two minutes and it is the last time anyone needs to measure you. Nothing goes anywhere else. Nobody else sees it. You can delete it the moment your profile is done. Camera on or off for the call itself, your choice. I need to see the clothes, not you.

    I’m an awkward size. Long arms, short legs, a chest that needs a large and a waist that doesn’t.

    That’s exactly who this works best for. Once you know your numbers, an awkward size stops being a problem and turns into a filter.

    My closet’s a state and I’d rather nobody looked in it.

    Good. A tidy closet tells me nothing. I’ve looked inside two hundred of them and never once been surprised. Don’t tidy. Don’t lay anything out. Don’t buy something first. Open the doors as they are.

    Can forty-five minutes really do this?

    Yes, because the hard part happens before we speak. Your fit and your colours are worked out already, so the call goes on you and your actual closet. You’ve spent longer than that in one shop putting things back on the rail.

    I’ll sort this out when I’m further along.

    Every man I sit down with says a version of that. Then he tells me people take him less seriously until he looks the part. So which comes first? Men don’t buy this after they’ve made it. They buy it before, which is why they get treated like they already have.

    So I bin most of what I own and buy it all again?

    No. Nothing has to leave your closet unless you want it to. You do not have to buy a thing. You will still be better off on Monday than you were on Friday. The four cuts mostly change which of your own things you reach for and in what order. What to buy next is a list you keep, not a bill you pay. You order off it when you were going to buy something anyway, which you were.

    Is the call a pitch for something bigger?

    The forty-five minutes is finished work on its own. You come off it with your numbers, your colours and everything you own sorted. If you never speak to me again, all of that still stands. There is more I can do with a man after that. At the end I will tell you what it is, in one sentence. You can ignore it and nothing you were given goes away.

    I’m not in the UK.

    Doesn’t matter. It’s a video call and the booking page shows times in yours. The scan works anywhere. Your measurements come in centimetres and inches both.
